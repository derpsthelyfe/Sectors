#Stub

As per 5e spellcasting rules, spells can have a verbal, material, and/or somatic component. To use material or a somatic component, you must have a free hand to access/perform the component. You **MAY NOT** use the same hand to satisfy both components (unlike base 5e)

Notable examples:
```
Hand 1: Open (may perform somatic xor material component)
Hand 2: Shield

This loadout lets clerics and paladins cast any spell (as they may put a holy emblem [their spellcasting focus] on the shield) but druids can't cast V+S+M or S+M (assume whenever I say "M", I mean material components that can be replaced with a spellcasting focus) spells since they can't use the shield as a spellcasting focus like the cleric can.
```

```
Hand 1: Open (may perform somatic xor material component)
Hand 2: Quarterstaff

Druids/arcane can cast any spell (as they may use the quarter staff as their spellcasting focus and use the other hand for somatic components) while cleric can't cast V+S or S+M
```
