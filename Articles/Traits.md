Traits are tags on [[Equipment]] that gives abilities. Traits are either inherent or they can be put onto equipment via a [[Catalyst]]. 

Trait abilities are sorted into two categories: trait effects and [[Set Bonuses]]. Some trait effects are only accessible in conjunction with other traits. Trait effects that specify a combination only check if any of your equipment has the associated trait, and it does not care if the prerequisite traits come from different equipment. If any two trait effects share the same activation conditions, you may only choose one to be activated.
