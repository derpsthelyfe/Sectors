#Terms #Stub 

Catalysts are aspects of the [[The Fey (character)|The Fey]] manifest to bless items. These items gain different effects based on what type of catalyst is manifested into the item; these can include demonic, celestial, fey abyssal, Beast, dragon, undead, primordial or primal. Catalysts are the manifestations of stories about the certain types of creatures for example many adventures telling stories about dying to dragons may form a draconic catalyst  

# Equipping Catalysts 
 When enchanting a armor, weapon, ring, or pendant (armor) you may take an additional step in order to imbue your catalyst when you do so you must increase the enchanting DC by another tier so a uncommon item would be treated as a rare item ( rare to very rare etc.) if the item is Artifact the DC is increased by 5. If you fail the enchanting check the item is destroyed and the catalyst returns to you. If you succeed you retain some of the magic of the catalyst allowing you to spend the necessary resources to recreate the catalyst (this will be explained later)
