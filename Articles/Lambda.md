# Primer
Lambda was [[Tundra]]'s second [[Season 6]] character after [[The Void Tornado]]. She is a [[Doll of Obsession]] who was born in the [[Doll House]]. She was a [[Drugs]] addict who used [[Chronomancy]] to get more drugs in her system. Near the end of [[Season 6]] she became a slave to [[Umeko]] and played a minor role in [[The Saving of Miko]].
