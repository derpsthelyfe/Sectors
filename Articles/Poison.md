in:[[Chemical Weapons]]


Poison is a general term that refers to natural substances and chemical weapons that are toxic to organic lifeforms. Poison can come in different forms and phases, and it can have various effects on the victims, depending on its composition. This can range from illness, paralysis to death. Poison can be derived from a wide range of sources, including being created by chemical engineering, or secreted naturally from [[Articles/Animal Species|animals]] or [[Articles/Plants and Fungi|plants]] as a defense mechanism.
