Class:

Starting at 3rd level your hunting has become as ruthless as the winter itself. You gain additional benefits when damaging Hostile creatures that are suffering from certain conditions as seen on the table below  
  
Condition Effect  

Prone
Your recover hitpoints equal to your Wisdom mod  
  
Poisoned
You gain advantage on your next saving throw against the target  
  
Frightened
The Creature can no longer take the disengage action until the start of its next turn.  

Grappled
Your attacks deal an additional 1d8 damage  
  
Paralyzed
If the attack kills a creature within 5 ft of you may choose an amount of allies equal to your wisdom mod they may use there reaction to move there movement without provoking attacks of opportunity  
  
Stunned
Until the start of your next turn you can take the dodge action as a bonus Action  
  
Blinded
You gain temporary hit points equal half your ranger level + wisdom Mod  
  
Deafened
The targeted creature cannot use dispel magic or counter spell for d4 turns
