Heavy whip of suffering  
2d4 + Dex mod + 3  
Reach: 15ft  
(Two-handed, Heavy, Reach, Finesse)  
  
Whirlwind attack: When you attack with this weapon this attack does (3) damage to all other hostile creatures within this weapon's reach.  
  
Constant Suffering: When you attack with this weapon on a miss this weapon may still deal it's poison damage however the creature is treated as passing the saving throw  
  
Precise Défense: when you attack a creature that is exactly your weapons base reach you may increase the damage dice by 1.
