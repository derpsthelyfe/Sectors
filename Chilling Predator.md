Class: Ranger

Starting at 3rd level when you cast the hunters mark spell. This spell may deal cold damage instead of necrotic. And you gain the following effect. When a creature fails a check or saving throw of a stat you cursed with hex you may use this ability if you do roll a d4 that creature now also suffers from the condition rolled until the start of its next turn. You may use this ability an amount of times equal to your wisdom mod per Long rest.  
  
  
Roll Condition  
1. Prone  
2, Poisoned  
3, Deafened  
4, Blinded
